Privacy Policy

Effective: November 2026
Medical malpractice conversations are highly sensitive. We take privacy seriously. Your conversation goes only to the law firms you are connected with, and only after you ask to be connected and agree to it in the contact form.

What We Collect

When you use NYCMalpractice.ai, we collect:

  • Your conversation messages (to provide AI responses and assess your situation)
  • Your name, phone number and preferred time to call, if you give them in the contact form or type them into the conversation, and a record of when you agreed to be contacted
  • Session identifiers (stored in a browser cookie named malpractice_session)
  • Basic usage data (timestamps, case type classifications)

We do not collect your medical records, insurance information, or any data you do not voluntarily provide in conversation.

Sensitivity of Malpractice Conversations

Conversations about medical malpractice often involve sensitive personal health information, descriptions of injuries, and details about deceased family members. We treat this data with the highest level of care. Your conversation data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest in our database.

How We Use Your Data

  • To provide AI-powered legal information and case assessment
  • To generate a lead quality score if your situation suggests a viable malpractice claim
  • To connect you with a law firm, if you request it
  • To improve our AI models and response quality (in anonymized, aggregated form only)

Data Sharing

Nothing about your case goes to a law firm unless you use the contact form and agree to be contacted. When you do, law firms in our marketplace receive a short summary ("preview") of your case that contains no name, phone number or other identifying details, only the nature of the claim, the injury and an approximate timeline. The firm that wins the auction for your case, or for cases offered at a fixed price each matching subscribed firm, also receives your name, phone number, preferred time to call and your conversation, and may contact you by phone or text. Firms pay us for these referrals.

Apart from the referral to law firms described above, we do not sell or share your data, and we never share it for marketing.

Data Retention

Client session conversations are retained for 90 days after your last message so you can return to your conversation. After that, the conversation and the text of your messages are automatically deleted. Law firm lead records are retained for 3 years for billing and compliance purposes.

Cookies

We set one cookie of our own (malpractice_session) to identify your conversation across visits. It contains only a random identifier — no personal information — and expires after 90 days. We do not use advertising cookies. Our analytics provider keeps its own identifier in your browser, as described under Analytics below.

Analytics

We use Mixpanel to measure how the site is used. When you open the home page or the law firm registration page, and when you take certain actions there, your browser sends Mixpanel a record of it. Those actions are starting a session, switching between the "I Was Harmed" and "A Loved One Died" modes, sending a message, seeing a case analysis, and, for law firms, starting and completing registration and starting checkout. The record says which action it was and can include the mode you are in, the number of the message within the conversation, and the claim-strength rating and score shown in a case analysis. Mixpanel also collects standard technical details automatically, such as your browser and operating system, screen size, the address of the page you are on and of the page you came from, and an approximate location (city, region and country) that it works out from your IP address; Mixpanel states that it does not store the IP address itself. Mixpanel keeps a randomly generated identifier in your browser so that visits from the same browser can be recognized. It is kept in local storage, where it remains until you clear this site's data, or, if local storage is unavailable, in a cookie that lasts up to a year. We do not send Mixpanel the text of your conversation, your name, or your phone number.

Third-Party Services

  • OpenAI: Your conversation messages are sent to OpenAI to generate AI responses. OpenAI's privacy policy applies: openai.com/privacy
  • CourtListener: Case law searches use the CourtListener API (Free Law Project). To find relevant cases, our server sends CourtListener a few search keywords based on your conversation, which can include words you typed. It does not send your IP address or the conversation itself.
  • Stripe: Payment processing for law firm subscriptions. Stripe's privacy policy applies: stripe.com/privacy
  • Neon: Database hosting. Data is stored in Neon's PostgreSQL cloud database with encryption at rest.
  • Vercel: Hosts the site and, like any web host, receives the IP address and browser details of each visit. Vercel's privacy policy applies: vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Mixpanel: Measures how the site is used, as described under Analytics above. Mixpanel's privacy policy applies: mixpanel.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Google Fonts: Supplies the typefaces our pages use. Your browser downloads them directly from Google, so Google receives your IP address and browser details when a page loads. Google's privacy policy applies: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Twilio: Sends SMS verification codes to law firms during registration. Twilio's privacy policy applies: twilio.com/en-us/legal/privacy

Your Rights

You may request deletion of your conversation data at any time by emailing privacy@nycmalpractice.ai with your session ID (visible in your browser cookies). We will delete your data within 30 days.
