# Birth Injury Medical Malpractice in NYC: Do You Have a Case?

Birth injuries are among the most devastating and legally complex categories of medical malpractice. When a child is harmed during labor, delivery, or the immediate newborn period as a result of a healthcare provider's negligence, the consequences can last a lifetime — and the legal claims can involve millions of dollars in past and future damages.

## What Is Birth Injury Malpractice?

Not every birth injury is malpractice. Some birth complications are known risks of labor and delivery that occur even with optimal care. Birth injury malpractice exists when:

1. A healthcare provider deviated from the accepted standard of care during the pregnancy, labor, delivery, or newborn period
2. That deviation caused an injury to the baby or mother that would not have occurred with proper care

## Common Types of Birth Injury Malpractice

### Failure to Recognize or Respond to Fetal Distress
Fetal heart rate monitoring during labor is designed to detect signs of fetal distress. Failure to recognize abnormal fetal heart rate patterns, act promptly when patterns are identified, or order an emergency cesarean section when indicated can result in hypoxic-ischemic encephalopathy (HIE), a form of brain damage caused by oxygen deprivation.

### Delayed or Improper Cesarean Section
When a vaginal delivery poses an unreasonable risk to the baby or mother, a C-section should be performed promptly. Delays in deciding to proceed with a C-section, or delays in performing the procedure, can cause preventable brain injury.

### Improper Use of Forceps or Vacuum Extraction
Instrument-assisted deliveries carry inherent risks that are dramatically increased when forceps or vacuum are applied incorrectly, excessive traction force is used, or multiple failed attempts are made before proceeding to C-section. Injuries include brachial plexus injury (Erb's palsy), skull fractures, intracranial hemorrhage, and facial nerve damage.

### Shoulder Dystocia Mismanagement
Shoulder dystocia occurs when the baby's shoulders become stuck during delivery. Negligent management — particularly applying excessive downward traction — is a significant cause of Erb's palsy.

### Failure to Diagnose and Treat Maternal Complications
Complications in the mother including preeclampsia, gestational diabetes, Group B Streptococcus, and placental abnormalities can directly harm the baby if not properly identified and managed.

## Common Birth Injuries and Their Consequences

| Injury | Cause | Potential Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Hypoxic-ischemic encephalopathy (HIE) | Oxygen deprivation during labor | Cerebral palsy, developmental disability, epilepsy |
| Brachial plexus injury (Erb's palsy) | Shoulder dystocia mismanagement | Arm weakness, partial or full paralysis |
| Intracranial hemorrhage | Instrument trauma | Neurological impairment, seizures |
| Kernicterus | Untreated severe jaundice | Cerebral palsy, hearing loss |

## New York's Statute of Limitations for Birth Injury Claims

- **For claims brought on behalf of the child:** The statute of limitations is tolled until the child turns 18, subject to a 10-year outer limit from the occurrence.
- **For wrongful death of a newborn:** 2 years from the date of death.
- **For claims by the mother:** Standard 2.5-year malpractice limitations period.

These rules are complex — consult an attorney promptly even if you believe the deadline is years away.

## Evaluating Your Case

The key questions in any birth injury case:

1. Was there a deviation from the standard of care? (Requires medical expert review)
2. Did that deviation cause the injury? (Causation is often the hardest element)
3. What is the extent of the injury and the projected future care needs?

Birth injury malpractice cases are expensive to litigate — expert fees alone can exceed $50,000–$100,000. They are typically taken on contingency by attorneys who believe the case is strong enough to justify the investment.
