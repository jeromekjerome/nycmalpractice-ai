# New Orleans Medical Malpractice Lawyers: What to Look for When Choosing

Finding the right medical malpractice attorney in New Orleans is critical—Louisiana's strict 1-year prescriptive period and unique legal standards make local expertise essential. Here's what to look for when evaluating malpractice lawyers.

## Essential Qualifications to Verify

**1. Board Certification**
Look for attorneys certified in medical malpractice law by the Louisiana Bar Association. This indicates significant expertise and experience.

**2. Trial Experience**
The attorney should have experience trying medical malpractice cases, not just settling them. This strengthens settlement negotiations.

**3. Medical Expert Network**
The best malpractice attorneys maintain relationships with qualified medical experts needed to evaluate claims and testify at trial.

**4. Louisiana-Specific Knowledge**
Your attorney must understand Louisiana's unique legal landscape:
- The 1-year prescriptive period requirement
- Comparative fault rules
- Expert affidavit requirements
- Louisiana's damage caps (varies by type of injury)

**5. Firm Resources**
Medical malpractice cases are expensive and time-consuming. A well-resourced firm can afford expert witnesses, discovery, and investigation.

## Questions to Ask During Consultation

- "How many malpractice cases have you tried?"
- "What's your settlement/trial record?"
- "How do you evaluate whether a case has merit?"
- "What expert witnesses do you work with?"
- "How do you charge—contingency or hourly?"
- "What's your timeline estimate for my case?"
- "How frequently will you communicate with me?"

## Red Flags to Avoid

- Promises of guaranteed outcomes
- Pressure to settle quickly
- Attorneys who don't specialize in medical malpractice
- Lack of transparency about costs and timeline
- Reluctance to discuss your case openly

## Cost Considerations

Most New Orleans malpractice attorneys work on **contingency**, meaning you pay no upfront fees—they collect a percentage (typically 25-33%) of your recovery. Some complex cases may require expert fee contributions.

## The Importance of Local Expertise

Medical malpractice standards, expert availability, and judge preferences vary by location. An attorney familiar with New Orleans courts and Louisiana law is invaluable.

## Beyond the Lawyer: Support Services

The best firms offer additional support:
- Case management and communication
- Medical record analysis
- Expert physician consultations
- Ongoing updates throughout your case

## Make Your Decision

The right attorney combines legal expertise, trial experience, medical knowledge, and genuine commitment to your case.
