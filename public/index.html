⏱️ NY Statute of Limitations

Personal injury: 2.5 years from the act or omission, or from the last treatment for the same condition

Public hospitals: notice of claim within 90 days; lawsuit within 1 year and 90 days

Federal providers (VA and military hospitals, many community health centers): written claim to the agency within 2 years

Wrongful death: 2 years from date of death

Later deadlines: foreign object (1 year from discovery), missed cancer diagnosis (Lavern’s Law, up to 7 years), infancy (no more than 10 years). Act promptly.
