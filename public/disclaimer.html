Statute of Limitations — Critical Warning

The medical malpractice statute of limitations in New York (CPLR §214-a) is generally 2 years and 6 months from the act or omission. Special rules apply:

  • Continuous treatment doctrine: SOL is tolled while the same provider treats you for the same condition
  • Foreign object exception: No SOL when a foreign object is left in the body
  • Wrongful death: 2 years from date of death (EPTL §5-4.1) — shorter than personal injury SOL
  • Infancy toll: SOL does not run against patients who were minors at the time of the malpractice
  • Discovery rule: Limited applicability in NY malpractice — generally does not apply

This platform does not provide a definitive SOL analysis. The AI may flag SOL concerns based on what you tell it, but it cannot definitively determine whether your claim is timely. Only a licensed attorney can make that determination.
