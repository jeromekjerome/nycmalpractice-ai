# Baton Rouge Personal Injury Attorney: How to Choose the Right Lawyer

If you've been injured in Baton Rouge — in a car accident, a slip and fall, a workplace incident, or a case of medical negligence — one of the most important decisions you'll make is choosing the right attorney.

## Why Louisiana Personal Injury Law Requires Local Expertise

Louisiana operates under a civil law system rooted in the Napoleonic Code — the only state in the U.S. that does not follow common law. Key implications:

- **Comparative fault rules:** Louisiana uses a pure comparative fault system — recovery is reduced proportionally but not eliminated
- **One-year prescriptive period:** Louisiana has a one-year statute of limitations for most personal injury claims
- **No caps on general damages (in most cases)**

## What to Look for in a Baton Rouge Personal Injury Attorney

### 1. Experience in Your Specific Injury Type
Personal injury is a broad category — car accidents, slip and falls, medical malpractice, and workplace injuries each have different legal frameworks.

### 2. Louisiana State Bar Licensing and Good Standing
Verify the attorney is licensed in Louisiana and in good standing with the Louisiana State Bar Association.

### 3. Contingency Fee Structure
Most Louisiana personal injury attorneys work on contingency (typically 33% pre-suit, 40% post-suit). Clarify what is deducted before vs. after the contingency percentage.

### 4. Trial Experience
Insurance companies know which firms will actually go to trial. Ask: Have you taken cases to trial in East Baton Rouge Parish?

### 5. Resources to Litigate
Medical malpractice and catastrophic injury cases require significant resources: expert witnesses, accident reconstruction specialists, life care planners.

## Red Flags to Avoid

- Attorneys who solicit you at the hospital or accident scene — ethically prohibited in Louisiana
- Guarantees of specific outcomes or settlement amounts
- Pressure to sign quickly without reviewing the engagement agreement
