Nursing home abuse is a serious problem in Louisiana, where staffing shortages and inconsistent regulatory enforcement create conditions where neglect can go undetected for months. If your family member has been harmed in a Louisiana care facility, you have meaningful legal rights — and a window to act.

## What Constitutes Abuse and Neglect in Louisiana Nursing Homes?

- **Physical abuse:** Hitting, restraining, or physically injuring a resident
- **Neglect:** Failure to provide necessary care resulting in bedsores, dehydration, malnutrition, or untreated infections
- **Medical malpractice:** Medication errors, missed diagnoses, or failure to follow physician orders
- **Emotional abuse:** Threats, humiliation, intimidation, or isolation
- **Sexual abuse:** Any non-consensual sexual contact
- **Financial exploitation:** Unauthorized use of a resident's assets or benefits

## Louisiana's Unique Legal Framework

**Medical Review Panel:** Most medical malpractice claims in Louisiana — including nursing home malpractice — must first go through a Medical Review Panel before a lawsuit can be filed. The panel consists of three healthcare providers who issue a non-binding opinion. This process typically takes 12-18 months.

**Statute of Limitations:** Louisiana generally requires medical malpractice claims within **one year** of the date the injury was discovered. Filing with the Medical Review Panel tolls this deadline. Wrongful death claims must be filed within one year of death.

**Damages Cap:** Louisiana caps total medical malpractice damages at $500,000 per claim for covered providers. Not all nursing homes qualify as covered providers — some face uncapped liability.

## Signs of Neglect to Document Immediately

- Unexplained bruises, cuts, or fractures
- Stage 2 or higher pressure ulcers (bedsores)
- Sudden weight loss or signs of dehydration
- Frequent falls or fall-related injuries
- Resident appears fearful of certain staff members
- Medical records show gaps in care or unsigned treatment orders

Document everything with photographs, dates, and detailed notes. Request copies of all medical records immediately.

## How to File a Complaint in Louisiana

- **Louisiana Department of Health, Health Standards Section:** Regulates nursing home licensure and investigates complaints
- **Louisiana Long-Term Care Ombudsman Program:** Advocates for residents and investigates concerns
- **Adult Protective Services:** For cases involving abuse of vulnerable adults
